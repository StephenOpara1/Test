WHAT'S YOUR SKIN TYPE?

Identify Your Skin Type:
Understanding Your Unique Needs


Dry Skin
Dry skin feels tight, rough, and many show signs of flakiness or dullness due to lack of natural moisture and hydration
Oily Skin
Oily skin feels greasy to the touch amd oftens looks shiny, especially on the forehead, nose, and chin areas.
Sensitive Skin
Sensitive skin is easily irrated and can react to various products or environmental factors, often leading to redness, itching, or discomfort
Combination Skin
Combination skin typically has both oily and dry areas on the face, withsome parts appearing shiny and others feeling dry or tight, making it a mix of different skin typres.
Normal Skin
Normal skin feels neither too dry nor too oily, maintaining a balamced and healthy appearance with minimal blemishes or discomfort
